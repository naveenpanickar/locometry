THE MODEL

Get the job matched, made and moved.

Locometry connects businesses to industrial machine shop capacity — CNC, fabrication, machining, laser, injection molding, 3D printing, design, repair, assembly — every shop vetted for capability, matched by distance first. Every job moves through three layers before it ships.

01 — MATCH

Fit the job to the shop

Drawings, tolerances, material, quantity and deadline get matched against what a shop can actually run nearby — not just what its listing claims.

People · Materials · Machines · Makers · Movers
02 — MAKE

Coordinate production

One part, one or many processes. We line up whichever shops need to touch it, so multi-step jobs move as one order, not five.

Design · Machine · Fabricate · Repair · Assemble
03 — MOVE

Get it to your door

Pickup, consolidation and delivery, arranged once the part is ready.

Pickup · Transport · Delivery · Deployment
HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, one network.

STEP — REQUEST

Send us the job

Drop in a CAD file — STEP, STL, SLDPRT, DXF and more — plus tolerances, material, quantity and deadline. No drawing yet? Tell us what needs designing, building, repairing or assembling and we'll scope it.

STEP — CONNECT

Get matched to a shop nearby

Matched on what the job actually needs — design, machining, fabrication, repair or assembly — not just what a shop's listing claims. We broadcast inside your radius first, and widen only if nothing nearby can meet it.

STEP — SHIP

Compare, order, it's made and moved

Quotes tagged with real distance and drive time. Pick on price, lead time, or the shop on your way home — then production runs as one coordinated order, however many steps it takes, with pickup or delivery once it's ready.
